Happy Birthday, William.

Deep within the darkness of Hawkins, Indiana,
a birthday secret is being kept from you.

But before it can be revealed,
you must prove you belong in the Party.

Three clues. Three answers. One surprise.

Clue 001 — The World Below
I exist alongside your world — a dark reflection of everything you know. No warmth. No colour. No hope.

Creatures from my depths have terrorised Hawkins for years. Will Byers was dragged into me against his will. Eleven reached through me to find the lost and the missing.

I am beneath your feet right now, in the dark, waiting. What am I called?
Clue 002 — She Has No Name
She was never given a birthday. She was never given a name.

Raised in a cold, windowless laboratory, her mind was trained as a weapon. She can slam doors without touching them, flip vans with a single thought, and reach into other worlds with her eyes closed.

She bleeds when she pushes too hard. Her friends call her El. Dr Brenner called her his. He gave her a number instead of a name. What was that number?
Clue 003 — Final
This is your final clue. Read it carefully.

Every monster has an origin. Every darkness has a beginning. Long before Will Byers disappeared into the dark, long before any Gate was torn open beneath Hawkins, long before anyone knew what lurked beneath their feet —

Something came first.
Something cast the very first shadow over everything that followed.

Its name tells you exactly what it was. What was it called?
